## About me

I am an Associate Professor at the Institute for Mathematical and Computational Engineering (IMC) of the Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile (UC) and Director of the [Magíster en Ciencia de Datos](https://online.uc.cl/programas/magister-en-ciencia-de-datos/) (UC Online). My research interests are Numerical Analysis and Scientific Computing. 

### Education
